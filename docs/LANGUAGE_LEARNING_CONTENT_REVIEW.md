## Editorial decisions

- Replaced project templates and descriptions in English, Portuguese and French.
- Focused on the learner's journey from activity to progress, the author's contribution, reusable activity types, completion rules, accessibility and QA.
- Retained a conceptual diagram near the start, using existing theme variables and the trusted SVG registry.
- Presented WCAG 2.1 AA as a design/testing target, not a certification or claim of universal compliance. Automated tests do not establish complete accessibility coverage.
- Removed exact completion triggers, simulation validation mechanics, test-user reuse details and pipeline naming conventions. Retained illustrative completion scenarios without prescribing internal business rules.
- Kept AI-assisted activities as platform context, without implying that the author designed an AI model or naming an unspecified provider.
